About Ms. Darkchild

The Princess
of Comedy

"I'm where I am suppose to be. My journey my story."

— Anissa Bane, @datgirlcomedy

Anissa Bane — A.K.A. Ms. Darkchild has commanded stages across Philadelphia since 1999. Comedian, host, actress, producer, artist manager, and founder of Absolute Soul Entertainment LLC.

Featured on FOX 29 Philadelphia. Actress in "The Whispering Bench" (role: Renee Brown). Developing original stage play "Tenant Eviction."

  • 1999
    Started comedy Saffron House of Comedy — weekly host segment
  • 2001
    Added hosting Expanded to full multilateral entertainer
  • 2012+
    Major co-bills Michael Blackson, Cocoa Brown, Rodney Perry & more
  • 2026
